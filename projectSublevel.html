Project 01 / Level design

Sublevel

Horror exploration · Level design · Unreal Engine 5.6

Sublevel is a horror game set inside an abandoned bunker, and your goal is to escape. I worked on the level design, environment, and lighting for this project in UE5.6.

My contribution
Level design, environment, lighting
Project focus
Horror and exploration
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.6

01 / Layout

Making a blockout

As I was in charge of the level design, I first wanted to make a blockout of the level to help me visualize and put something on paper so we could discuss the flow of the game and the overall feel.

Using Figma to map out a layout

I first used Figma to come up with some initial layout concepts. This was based on the brainstorm session I had with the team. I wanted the map to be big enough to make it feel like a maze, or at least certain parts of it.

Start of the blockout in Unreal

After talking with the team, we decided on the smallest version of the layouts I had made. This was due to time-management purposes, since we only had six weeks for the project.

02 / Level progress

From blockout to details

After making the first layout in Unreal, I built the rooms in three steps. First, I made simple versions of the rooms to get a feel for their scale and size. Then I added props to give each room its own purpose. For the final version, I worked on the lighting and post-processing to bring the atmosphere together.

1 of 3

Experimental room

This is a room where they conducted a series of experiments. In this room, there is a keycard you need to get in order to continue further into the game. I wanted it to have a cold atmosphere that contrasted with the warm lighting of other areas to highlight how important the room was.

1 of 3

Office

The office is not a mandatory room to beat the game; rather, it is a room that tells you more about the bunker, the story behind it, and what happened there.

1 of 3

Laboratory

To get to the laboratory, you need to have the keycard from one of the earlier rooms. Once you go in, there is no going back. From there on out, you are in the final part of the game, where a monster is lying on the operating table but wakes up once you walk too far into the room.

1 of 3

Warehouse

In the final room of the game, you need to survive for one minute while being hunted by the monster from the laboratory. Once you survive for one minute, the doors will open and you can escape the bunker and beat the game. I wanted the atmosphere of this room to feel tense and stressful.

03 / Modular hallways

Building a modular kit

I made a modular kit of all the hallway components to make the level design faster and more flexible. When I first placed all the hallways, it still felt empty and boring, which is why I added pipes and cables to give the map and the game more life.

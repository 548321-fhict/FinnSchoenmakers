Designing places
worth getting lost in.

Hi, I'm Finn — a game design student at Fontys University of Applied Sciences. I'm drawn to the spaces between things: how a path pulls you forward, how light hints at a story, and how a world can feel alive before a single word is spoken.

I work across level design, UI, and VFX, and I'm exploring 3D art and environment creation. I learn by building, testing, and making the next version a little better.

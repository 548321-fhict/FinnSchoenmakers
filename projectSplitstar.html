Project 02 / Gameplay

Project Splitstar

2.5D shooter · Level design · Visual effects · Unreal Engine 5.6

Splitstar is a combat-focused 2.5D shooter where players fight through multiple rooms and transform between two different drones. I worked on the level design and visual effects.

My contribution
Level design, environment, visual effects
Project focus
2.5D room-based shooter
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.6

01 / Layout

Planning the room sequence

I began with a room-by-room plan. I wanted the first few rooms to introduce new ideas and teach the game controls step by step.

1 of 6

From plan to blockout

I started by mapping out the level in Figma. This helped me show the group what the level could look like and gave me a better workflow: I could plan it out first instead of making decisions on the spot in Unreal Engine.

02 / Level design

Building rooms for combat

I divided the level into rooms with distinct gameplay purposes. Tighter spaces create pressure, while open arenas encourage players to focus on dodging rather than hiding behind cover. The layout stays readable while keeping players moving. The game follows a drone infiltrating a spaceship to destroy its core. We did not have time to implement every feature we wanted, but we were happy with the result.

1 of 4

Introduction

This room introduces the core mechanics and basic combat. I kept the layout simple so players can learn the controls, get comfortable moving through the level, shoot enemies, and try out the different drone forms.

1 of 4

Vertical gameplay

This room focuses on upward movement and avoiding hazards. Players must dodge lasers, enemies, and bullets, including attacks from turrets and pursuing explosive enemies.

1 of 4

Combining movement styles

This space combines different movement styles and asks players to use what they learned in the previous rooms. Players must defeat all the enemies to progress, and a counter shows how many remain.

1 of 4

The Gauntlet

This is the first real challenge of the game: a long stretch of varied combat encounters, from dashing through lasers to fighting swarms of enemies. Enemies can drop short-lived power-ups, such as a movement speed boost, increased fire rate, or health regeneration.

1 of 4

Boss arena

We planned a boss fight for the final level, but time constraints meant we had to simplify it. We made the turret enemies more challenging by giving them more health and damage.

03 / Visual effects

Making VFX for Splitstar

Alongside level design, I worked on the game's visual effects, mainly focusing on the weapons. I wanted these effects to help players better understand the gameplay and feel more immersed in the game.

Muzzle flash and bullet impact

The muzzle flash and bullet impact give players visual feedback when they fire and their bullets hit a target. This was my first time using Unreal Engine's Niagara system. I like how much control and customization it offers while remaining relatively simple to use.

Layered explosion effect

I built the explosion in stages. First, I adapted the bullet impact from the previous effect so the sparks flew in all directions. Then I created the smoke by copying the spark effect, disabling gravity, and changing the velocity so it stayed together. I used a similar approach for the fire and adjusted its properties to suit the effect.

Self-regulating lasers

I made these lasers to add an extra challenge. They use a line trace to adjust their length to fit the room, and periodically switch on and off so players can pass through without making the challenge too easy.

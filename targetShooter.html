Project 03 / Game jam

Target Shooter

Aim trainer · Game jam · Unreal Engine 5.6

I made Target Shooter on my own during a one-week game jam. It is a small aiming minigame where you shoot moving targets and try to get a high score.

Project focus
Aiming minigame
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.6

01 / Gameplay

A small prototype built around feel

I wanted the shooting to feel quick and clear, so I used a raycast to have hits register right away. I added moving targets and hit feedback to make the minigame more fun to play. Since I worked on it for only a week, I kept the idea small and focused on getting the main mechanics working.

The target range

I made a small target range for the minigame. The targets stand out against the wooden environment, giving players something more interesting to look at than a plain grey background.

Aiming and target movement

I added moving targets to make aiming a bit more challenging. When you hit one, it reacts straight away so you know your shot connected. The goal is to get the highest score possible. I left projectiles out of the equation to make the experience more direct.

How the targets work

This Blueprint controls how the targets move and react when they get hit. It also updates the score when you hit a target.

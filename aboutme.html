About me

Hi, I’m Finn Schoenmakers. I’m 21 and live in Venlo. I study Game Design at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, where I create interactive experiences using engines such as Unreal Engine and Unity.

I enjoy building and experimenting, especially with level design, UI, and VFX. I want my work to feel smooth, intuitive, and engaging for the player. I learn best by doing: prototyping an idea, testing it, and improving it step by step.

That hands-on approach carries into my team and solo work. On projects such as Sublevel and Project Splitstar, I’ve contributed to level design, UI, and visual effects. I also make solo projects to explore mechanics in more depth and strengthen my technical skills.

My interest in making games grew from a lifelong love of playing them, especially competitive shooters, survival games, and sandbox experiences. I became curious about how these games work, and that curiosity eventually led me to create my own.

Away from development, I enjoy bouldering and finding new ways to challenge myself. I also like spending time outdoors, especially in the mountains. At Fun Forest Venlo, I work as a climbing instructor and as part of the rescue team.

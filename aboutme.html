About me

Game design student · Creative development · Digital experiences

Hi, I’m Finn Schoenmakers. I live in Venlo and study Game Design at Fontys University of Applied Sciences in Eindhoven.

I’m interested in creating engaging games and interactive experiences. I enjoy taking an idea and thinking about how its different elements can come together to make something enjoyable, clear, and memorable for players.

At the moment, I’m especially interested in level design, 3D art, and environment creation. I like the mix of creative thinking and practical problem solving these areas involve, and I’m open to discovering other parts of game development as I continue to learn.

I’ve enjoyed playing games for as long as I can remember. Over time, I became curious about the ideas and work behind the games I played. That curiosity inspired me to study Game Design and start making projects of my own, both independently and with others.

Outside of my studies, I enjoy bouldering and spending time outdoors, especially in the mountains. I also work at Fun Forest Venlo as a climbing instructor and as part of the rescue team, where I enjoy helping people take on new challenges.
